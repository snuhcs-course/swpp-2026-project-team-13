## Selection and verification

- Examined all 13 downloadable MusicXML source collections containing pitched material listed on the source website; excluded the rhythm-only Smey collection.
- Retained one-part, one-staff pitched melodies with no chords, overlapping written pitches, unpitched notes or additional active voices.
- Main group: exactly 8 written measures, each containing its full notated meter.
- Pickup supplement: 9 written measure containers, consisting of an opening pickup, 7 complete measures, and a complementary shortened final measure; total duration equals 8 bars.
- Five exact musical duplicates were omitted. Transpositions are retained as distinct practice items; this is not a claim of 1,047 distinct tune families.
- Excluded Cole-Lewis-1904_2-03-221 and Somervell-1911_429 because a pitch in their native MIDI disagrees with their native MusicXML.
- The source MP3 files for Crowell-2004_293, Crowell-2004_294, Crowell-2004_295 and Crowell-2004_296 were corrupted, including the individual direct downloads checked again on the collection date. These four melodies retain XML, MIDI and PNG only. There are 1,043 playable MP3 files and 1,047 files in each of the other three formats.
- For all retained items, MIDI melody note-on pitches match the MusicXML sequence after tied continuations are combined. Some native MIDI performances have small articulation overlaps at note transitions; the written scores remain monophonic.
- PNG image integrity, file signatures, XML parsing, file presence, transcoded MP3 duration and ZIP checksums were checked. The entire collection has not been manually re-transcribed or listened to note by note. MP3s are compressed versions of the source recordings, not newly composed, MIDI-synthesized anew or independently pitch-transcribed.
- Original errors may remain; the checks above establish structure and XML/MIDI pitch agreement, not an independent proof of all engraving or audio details.
